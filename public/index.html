Frequently Asked Questions

Insurance questions, answered plainly

What does P&C (property & casualty) insurance actually cover?
Property & casualty insurance protects the things you own — your car, home, condo, rental property, or business — from covered losses like accidents, theft, fire, and weather damage, and it protects you (casualty) from liability if you're found responsible for someone else's injury or property damage. Auto, home, renters, umbrella, and business insurance all fall under the P&C umbrella.
How much is car insurance in Scottsdale, AZ?
Auto insurance rates in Scottsdale depend on your driving record, vehicle, coverage limits, and bundling discounts, so there isn't a single answer. The fastest way to get an accurate number is a free, no-obligation quote — most requests get a same-business-day response.
What is the minimum auto insurance required in Arizona?
Arizona requires liability coverage of at least $25,000 per person / $50,000 per accident for bodily injury and $15,000 for property damage (25/50/15). Minimums change over time and rarely provide enough protection after a serious accident, so we typically recommend higher limits — ask us to walk through the options for your situation.
Do I need flood insurance in the Scottsdale desert?
Standard homeowners policies generally exclude flood damage, and Scottsdale's monsoon season can bring fast, heavy flash flooding even in a desert climate. If your property sits near a wash, low-lying area, or flood-prone zone, a separate flood policy is worth reviewing.
Can I bundle my home and auto insurance for a discount?
Yes — bundling home (or renters) and auto policies with Farmers can qualify you for a multi-policy discount, and adding umbrella or other lines can unlock additional savings. Exact discounts depend on your policies and eligibility, which we'll confirm when we build your quote.
Does home insurance cover monsoon storm and hail damage in Arizona?
Most standard homeowners policies cover sudden wind and hail damage, which are common during Arizona's summer monsoon season, subject to your policy's terms and deductibles. We can review your current deductibles and roof coverage before storm season.
How fast can I get an insurance quote?
Submit the quote form on this page with a few details about what you're insuring, and a local licensed agent will follow up — typically the same business day — with personalized options.
What is umbrella insurance and do I need one?
Umbrella insurance adds an extra layer of liability protection above your home and auto policy limits, covering large claims or lawsuits that exceed those limits. It's often affordable and worth considering if you own property, have savings to protect, or carry higher personal liability risk.
