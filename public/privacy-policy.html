Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

CJ Browning Insurance Agency ("we," "us," "our," or the "Agency") is an independently owned and operated Farmers Insurance agency based in Scottsdale, Arizona. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information we receive through this website (cjbrowning.cjbrowning7.workers.dev, the "Site") and in the course of providing insurance products and services.

This Privacy Policy is intended to serve, in part, as our Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) privacy notice, which insurance producers are required to provide to consumers regarding the collection and sharing of nonpublic personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information about you:

  • Information you give us directly — such as your name, phone number, email address, mailing address, ZIP code, date of birth, driver's license number, vehicle information, property information, and details about your current insurance coverage, submitted through our quote form, by phone, email, or in person.
  • Information from your transactions with us — such as policy numbers, coverage selections, premiums, claims history, and payment information.
  • Information from consumer reporting agencies and other third parties — such as motor vehicle records, credit-based insurance scores, prior loss/claims history (e.g., CLUE reports), and public records, where permitted by law and used to underwrite or service a policy.
  • Information collected automatically — such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited on the Site, typically through standard web server logs and cookies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide insurance quotes and respond to your inquiries;
  • To place, service, and renew insurance policies on your behalf with Farmers Insurance and its affiliated carriers;
  • To process claims and assist with claims advocacy;
  • To communicate with you by phone, text/SMS, email, or mail about your quote, policy, or account, consistent with any consent you provide;
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and insurance industry recordkeeping requirements;
  • To improve the Site and our services.

Applicable law may give you the right to limit some sharing of your nonpublic personal information with nonaffiliated third parties for purposes other than servicing your policy or as otherwise permitted by law. To exercise this right or ask questions about our information-sharing practices, contact us using the information in Section 8 below.

4. Marketing Communications & TCPA Consent

If you submit the quote form on this Site, you may be asked to confirm that you agree to be contacted by phone, text message, or email regarding your request. Message and data rates may apply, message frequency varies, and consent to receive marketing communications is never a condition of purchasing insurance. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you may ask to be placed on our internal do-not-call/do-not-email list at any time by contacting us.

5. Cookies & Automated Data Collection

The Site may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and to understand how visitors use the Site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may limit some Site functionality.

6. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal and policy information for as long as needed to service your insurance needs and as required by Arizona insurance recordkeeping requirements and other applicable law.
